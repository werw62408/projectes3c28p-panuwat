HOW TO PLAY
  1. Walk. Your hooks lock on to the closest enemy inside the ring and strike by themselves. Hooks cannot pass rock walls.
  2. Every kill makes one stat grow. The icon over an enemy shows which one.
  3. Clear a zone, then stand in it until the ring fills: the zone is yours. You heal fast there, and you come back there if you fall.
  4. Big zone numbers are harder. Bosses give one more hook (up to 4).
