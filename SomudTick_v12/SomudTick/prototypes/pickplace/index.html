HOW TO PLAY
  1. Tap a feeder tube in the middle: its front part drops onto the belt. Only the front parts show; the rest is hidden.
  2. A broken device grabs the parts it asks for as they pass.
  3. Fixed devices power up and leave; the next one steps in.
  4. The belt holds a limited number of parts. Go over it and the belt jams.
