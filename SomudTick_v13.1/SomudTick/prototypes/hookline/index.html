HOW TO PLAY
  1. Walk. Your hooks lock on to the closest enemy inside the ring and strike by themselves. Hooks cannot pass rock walls.
  2. A red patch on the ground means a hit is coming there. Step out, or dash through it: a dash cannot be hit.
  3. Every kill makes one stat grow (the icon over an enemy shows which). Gold-edged elites give three times as much and always drop something.
  4. Clear a zone, then stand in it until the ring fills: the zone is yours. You heal fast there and come back there if you fall. Raiders try to take zones back: get there before the timer runs out.
  5. Bosses give one more hook. Bosses and every 5 zones give a hook upgrade. Beat the Warden in the far corner to win, then try New Game+.
