HOW A GAME GOES

  • Boosts: pick 2 of 3 cards before the game.
  • Nest: mark tiles to dig, place rooms; builders bring food to build them.
  • Queen: queue eggs; builders feed her; 2 queen-room tiles = 1 slot.
  • Outside: scouts clear the fog, loaders bring food, guards protect.
  • Raids: point at a base → SEND RAID. Bases drop sugar, chitin, cards.
  • Squads: group 2–12 ants and order them: patrol an area, guard a spot, walk a trail, raid, guard the queen.
  • Workshop: chitin → plates → level ants up to 5.
  • Outside the game: deck, forge, perks, shop, queen path, almanac.
